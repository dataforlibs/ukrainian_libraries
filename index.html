Source: OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL), Overpass snapshot . Points are element centres; polygons are reduced to a single point. This is what volunteers have tagged, not an official register: some objects carry no name, and some tags are loose (for example, an employment centre or a tax office tagged as a community centre). Borders: geoBoundaries (CC BY 4.0), simplified; Crimea, Sevastopol, Donetsk and Luhansk are shown as Ukraine, and hatching marks territory unlawfully occupied by Russia at oblast level (the front line is not drawn). Red rings mark libraries (and, in grey, a few historic churches in Lviv and Odesa, at approximate locations) individually documented in news reports as destroyed (solid) or damaged (dashed); oblast shading uses official Ministry of Culture counts, which are not tied to OSM objects, so the two are not the same dataset. There is no basemap, so grey labels mark a few towns. All data on this page are read from CSV files in the data/ folder published with it (also as one Excel workbook, war_damage_data.xlsx).
